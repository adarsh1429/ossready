# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. Everyone taking part in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other
project space is expected to follow it.

## In short

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's background or level of experience.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully; critique code, not people.
- No harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, or publishing others' private information.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer privately at
adarsh@dreabee.com. All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.
Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions, and may ban contributors
temporarily or permanently, as described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
